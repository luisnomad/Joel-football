All images were produced on 2026-07-29 with OpenAI's built-in image generation.
The four private photographs were attached as identity references but were not
copied into the repository.

## Identity anchor

Luis/Dad in the supplied photographs. Preserve his rectangular-oval face,
strong jaw, full shaped beard, thick straight eyebrows, deep-set brown/hazel
eyes, substantial straight nose, short textured wavy hair with tapered sides,
light warm skin, and calm half-smile.

Every hair on his head, temples, moustache, and beard must be rich natural dark
brown. Absolutely no gray, silver, white, blond, salt-and-pepper, or
desaturated hair, even where the photographs contain gray.

Match the polished, softly shaded 2D Joel Football style with clean dark
outlines and an expressive oversized head. Dress him in a deep navy V-neck
jersey with golden-yellow trim and royal-blue side panels, golden-yellow shorts
with navy trim, navy socks with gold bands, cream-and-navy football boots, and
a cobalt wristband. Show one complete three-quarter idle figure facing
screen-left on perfectly flat `#ff00ff` chroma, with no text, props, shadows,
floor, crop, or extra figure.

Dad is 6 feet 7 inches tall. Preserve the noticeably tall adult silhouette:
long legs, long arms, long torso, broad adult shoulders, and a smaller head
relative to the body than the compact roster characters. Do not shorten,
compress, chibify, or enlarge his head.

to the compact adult roster proportion. Luis explicitly rejected that
direction and approved the original tall anchor instead. The compact anchor and
sheet remain archived as `*-compact-v1*` provenance files and must not be used
as runtime sources.
