## H. Kickfall: engagement review (owner request; design decisions needed)

What it is: all 20 levels use one four-tier zigzag board. Difficulty comes from
parameters (quota, spawn rate, clock, gate health) and a few rules introduced
along the way (flat or uphill lanes, traps, a rival at levels 10 and 20). Every
level has the same objective: drain every ball before the clock runs out,
pass or fail.

Likely reasons it feels repetitive (to confirm in a playtest):

1. Same board shape every level.
2. Same objective every level, with no score, stars or reason to replay a level well.
3. Fiddly navigation for kids: changing lanes is "hold up or down and press Jump",
   and much of the time is spent chasing balls that roll away.
4. Kicks have little payoff: chains, cascades and big moments are not rewarded.

Proposals, roughly cheapest first:

| # | Proposal | Effort |
| --- | --- | --- |
| H1 | **Stars and score:** 1–3 stars per level for time left, gate-break combos and no wasted kicks, shown on a level-select map. Replay value at low cost. | S–M |
| H2 | **Combo and cascade rewards:** chain gate breaks within N seconds for a multiplier; bigger effects, slow-motion on a full cascade, crowd sound. | S |
| H3 | **Simpler navigation:** a single tap on Up or Down (or the lane end) transfers to the adjacent lane, with no jump combo; plus an optional "follow the ball" assist on Easy. | S |
| H4 | **Varied objectives per level:** a golden ball that must reach the drain first, breaking gates in a set order, a goal mouth on a tier (score N goals: a football tie-in), rescuing caged friends from the roster. | M |
| H5 | **Varied boards:** 3- or 5-tier layouts, split lanes, springs or trampolines, conveyors, moving gates; the spec already anticipates procedural remixing. | L |
| H6 | **In-level power-ups** that reuse the Power Lab powers (fireball kick breaks a gate in one hit, ice stops balls rolling back, Big Guy pushes a whole queue). Ties the mini-game to the math loop. | M |
| H7 | **More rival moments:** short rival appearances every few levels, instead of only at 10 and 20. | M |

**Status (batch 2):**

- **H1–H3 implemented.** Rules and thresholds are in
  [KICKFALL_SPEC.md](KICKFALL_SPEC.md#stars-and-combos-audit-h1h2-2026-10-08).
- Stars:
  - 1 star for clearing the level;
  - 2 stars for also finishing with at least 35% of the clock left;
  - 3 stars for also scoring a combo of 2+ or wasting at most 30% of kicks.
  - The best result per level is saved in the profile (sanitised). The total
    out of 60 is shown on the Mini-games Kickfall card.
- Combos chain gate breaks that land within 6 s of each other, capped at ×3,
  with a gold banner, shake and sound. The last gate triggers 0.6 s of
  0.4× slow motion.
- One-tap lanes: Up or Down moves one lane. Only straight joystick flicks
  count, so diagonals never change lane. Jump is always a free jump. Help text
  is updated in EN and ES.
- Captures are in `output/audit-batch-2/kickfall-rating/`.
- **Owner to confirm in a playtest:** the star thresholds, the combo window,
  and how the straight-flick lane change feels.
- **Not done:** the "follow the ball" assist on Easy (H3, optional) and a
  level-select map. Stars appear on the result panel and the card total.

**Status (batch 3):**

- **Level select:** a LEVELS ★ button opens a 20-tile picker on both the tablet
  and phone setup. Tiles show the best stars, locked tiles do nothing, the
  continue level is outlined, and tapping a tile starts that level. The overlay
  loads on demand, so the opening-payload contract holds. Escape and platform
  back close it. EN and ES strings are included.
- **Easy "follow the ball" assist:** on Easy only, a pulsing gold ring marks the
  ball to chase, and a chevron beside the player points left, right, up or down
  to it. It is visual only (`kickfallAssist.js`), and the Easy description says
  so.
- **Ball landing glow:** one cyan glow per ball on the deck below it.
- **AI own goals:** after the AI passed the human, it faced its own goal while
  retreating and kicked. Within 420 px of its own goal it now never kicks or
  lobs toward it, and steps goal-side of the ball instead (`HeuristicAgentProvider.js`).
- Captures are in `output/audit-batch-3/kickfall-levels/` and `kickfall-assist/`.
